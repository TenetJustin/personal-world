## Outdoor field recording

- **Park ambiences — park_ambience_birds.wav** by **Thimras**.
- Source: <https://opengameart.org/content/park-ambiences>
- License: CC0 1.0,
  <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.
- Runtime file: `public/audio/outdoor-birds.mp3`.
- Changes: a three-minute excerpt was loudness-normalized, loop-crossfaded and
  encoded to MP3. It remains a real 48 kHz stereo field recording; no synthetic
  bird sound was substituted for it.
